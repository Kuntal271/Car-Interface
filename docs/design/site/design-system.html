Foundations

Design system

Three colours and three typefaces, used with strict meaning. Cobalt means low, cold, idle and structural. Lime means high, hot, active, selected and warning. Nothing else is allowed.

The Ford theme: deep blue-black glass, Ford blue for structure, ice-cyan for live values, white for the primary reading, amber only for warnings. Rounded surfaces, Barlow throughout, and motion that only ever moves transform and opacity.

Colour rules

No red

Warnings are filled lime blocks with Midnight text, pulsing at 1 Hz. They are the only large lime areas in the system, so they are impossible to miss.

No blends

Gauges step from cobalt to lime at a threshold. There is no cobalt-to-lime gradient anywhere, so a colour always means one thing.

Never colour alone

Every state also has a shape, a label or a position: warnings carry an icon and words, selection carries a left bar, open doors swing out.

Ford theme rules

Blue is structure

Ford blue draws rings, bars and borders. Sky blue carries icons. Neither is used for small text.

Cyan is live

Only values that are changing right now (boost, peak, tuned station) use ice-cyan. The speed is white: the primary reading is never coloured.

Amber is the only alarm

Redline, door open, low battery and overheating use amber blocks with dark text. No red anywhere, and no colour is ever the only signal.

The Blue Oval

Use and limits

  • Official flat vector, bundled in assets/brand/. Shown in the status bar, Home, Standby and the boot animation.
  • Ford and the Blue Oval are Ford Motor Company trademarks. Personal use on your own car is fine; do not publish this site or app publicly with the logo in it.
  • The brand typeface (FordAntenna) is proprietary and not bundled. Barlow and Barlow Condensed (OFL) stand in: same industrial-humanist feel, not identical.
  • The oval's navy is lifted slightly (#08399A) so its edge reads on a dark screen.

How it stays smooth

  • Never animate blur, shadow, size or layout. Only transform and opacity on cached layers, so the GPU does no re-raster.
  • Glows, gradients and the ambient light are rendered once to bitmaps; per frame they are only moved.
  • Two quality tiers: Rich (everything above) and Lite (no stagger, no ambient drift, solid cards). The app measures frame time on the real unit and drops to Lite by itself if more than 2 % of frames exceed 12 ms.
  • The gate is measured, not promised: frame cost p99 at or below 10 ms with zero missed vsyncs over a 10 minute drive cycle, on the real unit.

Rules

  • The dock lives on the right edge: the car is right-hand drive, so it sits under the driver's hand. Settings can mirror it.
  • Touch targets are 64 px or larger; primary actions 80–96 px.
  • Gaps are 12 px between related cards, 24 px between columns.
  • Cards align to a shared top and bottom edge in each column.
  • Nothing important sits in the bottom 24 px.
Shape language

Chamfer

Sample
14 px cut

Cards cut the top-right corner by 14 px. Buttons cut 12 px. It is the signature shape and costs nothing to draw: one clip path.

Segments

Bars are separate slanted blocks, never a smooth fill. They read faster, and the slant echoes the chamfer.

Hairlines

Rowvalue
Rowvalue

1 px cobalt at 34 % separates everything. No shadows, no blur: both are expensive on a head-unit GPU.

Data states

Live

Value in full colour. Lime only when it is in its high range.

Stale

No update for 2 s: value dims to Dim colour and a STALE badge appears. Never show an old number as if it were current.

Unavailable

Shown as —, or the field is hidden if the car never provides it. Never a fake zero. Simulated data always carries a SIM badge.
