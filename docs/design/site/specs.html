Performance rules for the Compose build

Budget

  • Frame cost p99 ≤ 10 ms, zero missed vsyncs over a 10 minute drive cycle. Stretch: 5 ms.
  • No allocation in the draw phase of a gauge.
  • Cold start to interactive ≤ 4 s.
  • Measured with dumpsys gfxinfo on the real unit, not on the emulator.

Technique

  • Read gauge state inside drawBehind, so a new rpm redraws one layer and never recomposes.
  • Pre-build segment Paths once per size; per frame only choose the fill colour.
  • One StateFlow<VehicleSample>, sampled at display rate, shared by all screens.
  • Static backgrounds (grid, vignette, ambient glow) are cached bitmaps that are only moved. No blur, no shadows.
  • Two tiers, Rich and Lite. The app watches frame time on the unit and drops to Lite (no stagger, no ambient drift, solid cards) if more than 2 % of frames exceed 12 ms.
  • Ford theme tach: one sweepGradient arc, one drawArc per frame, tip as a rotated bitmap. Boot animation: pre-rendered, not computed at runtime.
